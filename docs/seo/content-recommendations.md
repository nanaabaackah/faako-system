# Public content recommendations

These are editorial inputs to collect, not invented copy.

## Across public sites

- Assign one owner and review date to each service, product, policy, case study, article and event.
- Add short answer-first sections for genuine visitor questions, then link to the detailed page.
- Record canonical organisation names, addresses/service areas, contact channels and social profiles once per brand.
- Maintain descriptive internal links between related services, categories, products, case studies and contact actions.
- For every claim, capture its evidence/source and review date. Do not publish unsupported superlatives, impact numbers, testimonials, client names or certifications.

## byNana

- Keep project status, responsibilities, technology and outcomes aligned with the live applications.
- Add measured outcomes only when analytics/client evidence is approved.
- Maintain article publish/modified dates and author information.

## Faako

- Clarify which modules are live, configurable, demo-only or planned.
- Publish concise security/data ownership answers and onboarding prerequisites from approved product documentation.
- Link each solution/industry page to an appropriate case study or contact/setup action.

## REEBS and Stroane

- Keep price, availability, category and product identifiers synchronised with the API/catalogue source.
- Add delivery, pickup, return/refund and enquiry answers only from approved policies.
- Use real product imagery/alt text and avoid duplicate descriptions across category/product routes.

## TTNGH before scaffold content is approved

Collect legal organisation name, registration details safe for publication, programme/service descriptions, support boundaries/crisis wording, verified contact channels, safeguarding/privacy policies, donation fund use, Paystack/MoMo merchant ownership, refund policy, event owners/dates, team consent, impact evidence and authentic image consent. The existing requirements and tagline are inputs, not proof of these facts.
