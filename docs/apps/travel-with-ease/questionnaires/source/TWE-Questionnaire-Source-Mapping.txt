# Travel With Ease — Imported Questionnaire Mapping

Source: two Google Forms JSON exports supplied on 2026-11-09. This is a source-grounded extraction and implementation mapping, not an approved production questionnaire. No customer responses were supplied.

## Executive findings

- **Full Trip Planning – Client Intake Form:** 43 items, 29 questions, 10 page-break sections, 4 section headers. 21 questions flagged required and 8 optional.
- **Start Here — Plan Your Trip with Ease:** 7 items, 3 questions, 4 page-break sections. One required and two optional questions.
- The first form covers two *planning-fee tiers* (Full Trip Planning; Premium Concierge), not individual destination travel package products.
- The second form is a *service chooser and explanatory page*; it is not the Travel Readiness client intake form.
- The exports do not provide live external link targets or full native Google Forms response validation rules.

## Implementation risks and checks

- **Do not use the fee tier as a destination package ID.** The Full Trip Planning and Premium Concierge choices refer to planning services, whereas TWE website travel packages have their own stable catalogue identifiers.
- **Missing source questionnaires:** No Travel Readiness Session intake questions, no per-destination package form, and no other specialist-service intake supplied.
- **Source text vs approved commercial terms:** GHS 400 Readiness Session, GHS 2,000 Full Trip Planning, GHS 3,000 Premium Concierge, deposits, add-on fees, timing, refund/cancellation language and booking promises must be checked against currently approved TWE CMS/policies before public release.
- **Branch analysis:** Confirm actual path via live form preview; the export represents native page navigation but the link targets in explanatory section descriptions may not be present. Verify both Yes/No visa paths and all router paths.
- **Checkbox constraints:** “Select up to 3” on accommodation priorities is only a help-text statement in the export, not an exported machine-readable validator. The five confirmations marked “all required” are in one checkbox question; `required: true` alone does not require all five. Encode explicit limits and per-declaration consent, subject to client-approved terms.
- **Sensitive data:** The dietary-requirements/allergies question asks for health-related details. Do not silently add this to a public intake or store it in generic enquiry details; obtain specific approval and implement appropriate minimisation/access/retention safeguards or defer to a private booking-stage process. Visa type/expiry can also be sensitive and should be minimised and access-restricted; never request passport numbers or scans in the public form.
- **Preserve original wording in source mapping** but distinguish original question content from new UI polish and formal approvals.
- **Validation:** Handle hidden fields on branching; preserve only applicable answers; enforce server-side validation with matching version; store question IDs, label snapshot, answers and source/version.
- **Security:** Maintain TWE tenant scope, auth for staff reads, rate limit, Turnstile, no leaked PII in query strings/logs, replay protection and notification safeguards.
