# Editing the Travel With Ease footer

1. Sign in to the TWE portal and open **Content**.
2. Choose **Footer** from the Section menu.
3. Edit the supporting text, contact details, section headings, social destinations, navigation links and legal information.
4. Use **Footer logo** to choose an approved image from the Media library.
5. Under image placements, choose the **Footer background image**. Desktop and mobile images, focal positions and alternative text use the same managed image controls as other website sections.
6. Save the draft and choose **Preview saved content**. This preview is private and does not change the public website.
7. Review link destinations and ordering. Hidden links remain stored but are not shown publicly.
8. Choose **Publish** only after review. The website changes only when deployment is verified and Publication history reports **Live**.

Existing footer values are offered when the dedicated Footer section is first opened. They are not published or overwritten merely by opening the editor. Footer revisions, failed publications and recovery remain in the normal CMS history.
